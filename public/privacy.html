Privacy Policy — In the Midst

Effective October 30, 2026.

What we collect

We collect the name you enter, the codes for gatherings you create or join, and the prayer requests and chat messages you post.

Calls

During a video gathering, your camera and microphone streams go directly between participants for the live call. We do not record calls and do not store call audio or video.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for an account or password, and we do not collect your contacts, your location, or advertising data. We show no ads and do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Storage

Gatherings and messages live on our server and may be cleared when the server restarts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@pheels.com.
